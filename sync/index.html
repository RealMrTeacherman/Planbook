Live sync for the planner

The planner and calendar sync instantly through your own Firebase account. They never hold student records; anything that can name a child stays on the Drive path below.

Live sync is off. It turns on once your Firebase settings are added to settings/firebase.js in the repo.

Sign in with the email and password you set up in Firebase. You do this once on each device.

Try live sync

Add a day off here. It appears on your other device within a few seconds, even if that device was offline until now.

There is no class list on this device, so labels here cannot be checked for student names. Load the MacBook's file to add one.

Watch a Drive folder

Do this once on the MacBook. In the window that opens, choose Google Drive in the sidebar, then a folder for the suite (make one called Planbook if you like).

Send to the MacBook

Tap Send, choose the Drive app in the share sheet, then the Planbook folder, and upload. The MacBook picks it up within a minute when it is open.

Load from the MacBook

First, in the Drive app, open the Planbook folder, tap ⋯ next to classroom-suite.json, then Send a copy, Save to Files, On My iPhone. Then tap Load here and choose it from On My iPhone.

Try it

Rename one math group here and a different one on your other device. Then send from the iPhone and load on it again. Both devices should show both new names.
