The MacBook and iPhone stay in step through a folder in your district Google Drive. Both work without a signal; changes travel when you send or load.

This device

Watch a Drive folder

Do this once on the MacBook. In the window that opens, choose Google Drive in the sidebar, then a folder for the suite (make one called Planbook if you like).

Send to the MacBook

Tap Send, choose Save to Files, then Google Drive and the suite's folder. The MacBook picks it up within a few seconds when it is open.

Load from the MacBook

Tap Load, then choose classroom-suite.json from Google Drive. If Google Drive is not listed, open Browse in the picker, tap ⋯, Edit, and switch it on.
